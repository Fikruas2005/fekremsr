Company

Agricultural science meets digital intelligence.

WonkaFarm Brain is a U.S.-based agricultural technology company developing decision-intelligence systems for crop production and agricultural research.

We combine crop modeling, agronomy, remote sensing, computer vision and AI into practical systems that move from field observations to management decisions.

Built on agronomic intelligence

We do not treat AI as a replacement for agricultural science. Our systems are designed around crop processes and agronomic mechanisms, with AI used to scale analysis, interaction and delivery.
