About WonkaFarm Brain
Building the Intelligence Layer for Agriculture
AI agricultural intelligence company · Products · Platforms · Specialist consultancy

WonkaFarm Brain is an AI agricultural intelligence company developing practical software, decision-support systems, and digital platforms for agriculture. We build tools that help farmers, research teams, governments, NGOs, agribusinesses, and AgTech startups turn crop data into better decisions.

The company brings together crop science, artificial intelligence, computer vision, crop modelling, remote sensing, drones, field sensors, and data engineering to make advanced agricultural intelligence easier to access and easier to use — from WhatsApp-based crop advisory to enterprise-scale digital agriculture systems.

Our mission is to make world-class agricultural intelligence accessible to every farmer and every organization. Our vision is to become one of the world's leading agricultural intelligence companies, transforming how agriculture is monitored, advised, modelled, and managed through AI and scientific innovation.

Leadership
Dr. Fekremariam Asargew Mihretie
Founder, Chief Executive Officer & Chief Scientist

Dr. Mihretie founded WonkaFarm Brain to advance AI-powered agriculture and make high-quality crop intelligence available beyond traditional research institutions. He leads the company's product direction, scientific strategy, and enterprise partnerships.

He brings 20+ years of international experience across Ethiopia, Ireland, Japan, Australia, and the United States, with expertise in crop modelling, digital agriculture, AI, remote sensing, drones, RGB imagery, field sensors, crop physiology, and agricultural decision-support systems. He holds a PhD in Global Dryland Science from Tottori University, Japan, and has authored 16 peer-reviewed publications.

Results Delivered

Crop Modelling · International Consultancy · Africa & Central Asia

Sustainable Cropping Systems for Dryland Farming — Morocco, Egypt, Uzbekistan

Designed sustainable soil management and cropping strategies for dryland farming contexts across multiple countries in Africa and Central Asia. Delivered hands-on crop-model training to multidisciplinary international research teams.

Outcome
Process-based crop-model scenarios delivered for contrasting dryland environments. Research teams trained for independent ongoing modelling. Full remote international consultancy completed on time.
📍 Africa & Central Asia🌍 International Consultancy
Crop Modelling · Legume Systems · Australia

Legume-Based Rotation Optimisation — South-Eastern Australian Cropping Systems

Led field experiments and crop-model simulation (APSIM Next Gen) on legume-based crop rotations — modelling nitrogen dynamics, soil carbon, profitability, and fertiliser-use efficiency across south-eastern Australian cropping systems under multiple climate scenarios.

Outcome
Crop-model simulation of nitrogen dynamics and yield outcomes across climate scenarios delivered. Findings provide evidence-based recommendations for commercial grain farmers across south-eastern Australia.
📍 South-Eastern Australia🌾 Legume-Based Systems
Water Stress · Crop Physiology · Japan · 2022–2023

Photosynthesis–Stomatal Conductance Under Progressive Drought in Rice

Investigated physiological mechanisms of rice response to soil water stress in Japan under controlled conditions. Quantified changes in the stomatal conductance–photosynthesis relationship under progressive drought.

Outcome
Published in Science of the Total Environment (2024). Findings improve drought-stress simulation in crop models — directly applicable to climate-smart agriculture globally.
📍 Japan📄 Published STE 2024
Smallholder Systems · Ethiopia · ARARI · 2005–2015

Smallholder Crop Management Programme — Ethiopian Highlands

Led a 10-year research programme at ARARI developing and promoting improved soil and nutrient management for Ethiopian highland crops — sorghum, wheat, maize, teff, finger millet. Transitioning smallholders to evidence-based nutrient management.

Outcome
Multiple publications in Field Crops Research, Agronomy Journal. Improved teff management recommendations adopted across the Amhara Region, benefiting thousands of smallholder households.
📍 Ethiopian Highlands🌱 Smallholder Systems⏱ 10 years
